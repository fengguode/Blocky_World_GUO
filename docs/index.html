Gameplay-distance observations and limits

  • At the matched first-person spawn view, the cobble path, grass, hut masonry, logs, foliage, flowers, and animals are present in both builds. The after view shows surface detail at this distance, with stronger flecks in the grass and wood/leaf texture.
  • Gold and Glowstone were placed temporarily in both preview pages on the path and inspected from a few blocks away. Gold reads as a warm ochre block with darker ore-like marks. Glowstone’s cyan field and bright star are easy to pick out under the current lighting.
  • The seed 1337 scene contains a flowing stream. From a lower free-camera view its blue surface is visible, but this run did not capture glass or ice at normal gameplay distance. Neither is available in the standard Play & Build hotbar or present in the sampled scene.
  • The preview remains labeled “fixed dawn camera” and keeps a purple sky/pink haze with strong teal-green ground tint. The DAY NOW control did not produce a verifiable daylight scene here. No warm-sunset capture was obtained, so lighting-dependent readability for glass, ice, ores, and emissive/decor blocks remains incomplete.

No bounded texture edit was justified by this sample: the tested gold and Glowstone were distinguishable, while the unobserved materials and lighting states need evidence before palette changes can be judged.
